🌞 NULLSTAR → The OS for Multiplayer Superintelligence

In which I introduce how I believe friendship networks will be done in the future.

💡 [EDIT; October, 2026] Dates have been defined; 37 4 life.


🌞 Hello, Starlit World


Today I'm excited to talk a little bit about what I've been cooking:

✨ Introducing NULLSTAR, an agentic-native game and intelligence studio — or as I like to call it, the operating system for collective superintelligence. ✨


What does that mean exactly? I've been building a seven-layer, vertically integrated infrastructure and operating system designed to fundamentally change how multiplayer games, friendship networks, and interactive experiences are created and delivered.

This operating system, with the prototype codename PleiadesOS, is designed to introduce advanced procedural content, ability, and world generation systems, enabling an unprecedented form of nonlinear storytelling through dynamic quests, branching outcomes, and evolving world states governed by flexible rules and alternative physics. Non-player characters (NPCs) are modeled as autonomous agents with persistent memory, goals, and decision-making capabilities. They adapt to player behavior, enabling players to co-create their experiences — all built on top of current industry tools and standards.


🌞 A Small Glimpse Under the Hood


My vision is to move gaming beyond static, developer-limited experiences toward scalable, intelligence-driven worlds built for the Age of Agents — and to bring every daring hero along for the ride.

Here is how PleiadesOS is currently being developed:


1. MAIA v1.0: A minimal harness designed to serve as the core of our graph-based infrastructure and vertical operating system. Persistent, adaptive, and self-improving by design, it features built-in guardrails, a robust middleware layer, intelligent model routing, and continuous verification loops. Written in Rust, this module is scheduled to be open-sourced in 2027.

2. ELECTRA v1.0: A multi-layered memory harness powering our knowledge factory. It manages short- and long-term memory, internal state and context, secure vaults and persistent storage, compaction and summarization, dreaming, as well as graph-based, high-performance search and intelligent retrieval. Written in Rust, this module is integrated with both MAIA and TAYGETE.

3. TAYGETE v1.0: An extensible, customizable, composable, adaptive, and self-improving harness wrapper written in Rust and Python. It enables agents to modify their own runtime and integrates our observability-driven adaptation engine. It also offers native support for a range of additional tools and optional capabilities, including monetization, multimodal integration, and advanced caching. The project is planned for a future open-source release.

4. ALCYONE v1.0: Evals, observability, benchmarking, testing, security hardening, compliance, and dashboards. Integrated across the operating system.

5. CELAENO v1.0: A novel multiplayer deployment and infrastructure layer built around the coordinated orchestration of sandboxed subagents. Its core, written in Rust, manages the agents' runtime message queues and schedulers and is integrated accross the operating system.

6. STEROPE v1.0: Where the game engines live. Integrated with MAIA and TAYGETE.

7. MEROPE v1.0: Proprietary NPC cognition engine. Integrated with STEROPE.
